# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People managing their own wardrobe from a phone who want useful outfit ideas from clothes they already own, especially before going out or while organizing their clothes.

## Product Purpose

Digital Closet helps people register garments, browse their wardrobe, and create outfit combinations for a chosen occasion or style. A useful suggestion can be understood, adjusted, saved, marked as worn, or rejected as an exact combination. Early pilot measures are hypotheses: adding a common garment in under 30 seconds after choosing a photo, saving or marking at least four of the first ten suggestions as useful, and returning to the product after two to three weeks.

## Brand Personality

Close, practical, and non-judgmental. The interface speaks Spanish in a direct, supportive tone. It does not pressure people to buy clothes or judge bodies or appearance.

## Anti-references

- A public social feed, public profiles, or social approval mechanics.
- A clothing store, sponsored recommendations, or purchase pressure.
- Virtual try-on, synthetic avatars, or claims of advanced image recognition.
- Appearance or body-shape judgments.

## Design Principles

- Keep the person in control: garment details and outfit combinations can be edited, swapped, saved, or rejected.
- Explain each suggestion briefly and let the person decide whether it works.
- Rejecting an exact outfit must not disable the garments it contains.
- Keep wardrobe photos private by default and avoid public sharing.
- Keep the first flow short: browse garments, request an idea, adjust it, and choose what to do with it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use WCAG AA as the baseline for the web preview, including readable contrast and keyboard-visible focus. Provide screen-reader labels and roles, comfortable touch targets, support font scaling, and respect reduced-motion preferences across supported platforms.
